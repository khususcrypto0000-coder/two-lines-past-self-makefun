# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Two Lines to Your Past Self"** ($2, winner-takes-all).

## The entry

> Don't trust the Elm Street meter Tuesday afternoon — it expired while you were still in the interview, and you came back to a ticket on the windshield.
> You ignored the fine for a month, so it doubled and ate a week's grocery money, and now you set a phone alarm for every meter.

Two lines, one specific moment: a parking meter on Elm Street on a Tuesday afternoon that ran out during a job interview. Line 2 traces the consequence — the ignored fine doubled and cost a week's groceries — and what changed: a phone alarm for every meter since.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_DIR`, e.g. `OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py`). The program asserts the entry is 1–280 characters, exactly 2 lines, one sentence per line, and verifies the written file is byte-identical on read-back.
